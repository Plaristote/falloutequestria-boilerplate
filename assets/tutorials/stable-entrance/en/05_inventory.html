Your people wouldn't send you out in the wild without some means of defense. Depending on the specialties you picked for your character, you should have received some equipment.

Let's open the inventory to see what's at your disposal. Press the I key or click the INV button to open your inventory.

When clicking on an item from the left side, you'll see a new section appear, showing a description of the item, as well as some actions you may perform directly from inventory. If hurt, you may want to use a health potion:

Let's see how to gear up your character. The fast use slots are what you'll need to use items during combat. Depending on your specialties, your inventory should contain either a gun or a knife. Drag and drop that item into one of the use slots:

That's it: you can now use the item to fight.
